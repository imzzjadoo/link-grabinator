# Link Grabinator: YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok & X Video and MP3 Downloader for Android

**The easy way to download music and videos from YouTube and other sites.** Paste a link from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X (Twitter) or any direct file link, then save it as an **MP3** or as a **video** in the quality you want.

## 📥 Download

👉 **[Get the latest version](https://github.com/imzzjadoo/link-grabinator/releases/latest)**

Most phones should use **LinkGrabinator-arm64.apk** (smaller). If it won't install, use **LinkGrabinator-universal.apk**.

## ✅ Supported sites

- **YouTube**: videos, music, Shorts and full playlists
- **Facebook**: videos and reels
- **Instagram**: reels and video posts
- **TikTok**: videos
- **X (Twitter)**: videos (see the note below)
- **Direct file links** to video or audio files
- **Spotify playlists**: shows the track list (Spotify's audio itself is protected and can't be downloaded)

## 🎵 Music or 🎬 video: your choice

- **MP3 audio**: turn any YouTube or social video into a song you can play in any music player. M4A is also available.
- **Video**: download the video itself and pick the quality you want.

## ⭐ Features

- Paste a link and download in seconds
- **Playlists**: look up a whole playlist, select all or pick the songs you want, and batch download
- **Sign in** to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and X for private or restricted posts
- Choose your own download folder
- One-tap **Update engine** to keep downloads working when sites change

## ℹ️ Good to know

- **X (Twitter):** many X videos only download after you sign in to X inside the app (Settings, then Sign in, then X). Use your X username and password. Google sign-in doesn't work inside apps, so if you normally use Google, set an X password first with "Forgot password?" on X.
- Private or age-restricted posts on other sites may also need you to sign in.
- If downloads suddenly stop working, tap **Update engine**.

## 📲 How to install

1. Download the APK on your Android phone.
2. Open it. If asked, allow your browser or file manager to "install unknown apps".
3. Tap **Install**.
